Start here

  1. Set up SideStore.

    Use the official prerequisites and installation guide. You’ll need a computer, USB cable, Wi-Fi, your Apple account and LocalDevVPN. The helper may require an App Store download.

  2. Refresh SideStore once.

    Finish Apple sign-in, follow the trust and Developer Mode prompts, then refresh SideStore with Wi-Fi and LocalDevVPN connected.

  3. Add RIVL.

    Save the IPA to Files. In SideStore, open My Apps → + and choose RIVL.ipa. Wait for signing to finish, then open RIVL.

How do I keep RIVL working?

Refresh both RIVL and SideStore before their seven-day counters expire, using Wi-Fi and LocalDevVPN. Check the countdown; background refresh can fail. Free accounts allow three apps including SideStore and ten App IDs per week. Pairing problems or iOS updates may need a computer again.

What is included in this preview?

The standalone iPhone app, without the Watch companion. The original Release opens on the development phone.
